Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

PublishKit stores your content library, tasks, and settings locally on your device. The desktop app does not upload your copy or media to our servers by default.

发稿匣 默认将内容库、任务与设置保存在你的本地设备，不会将你的文案或素材上传到我们的服务器。

Browser extension / 浏览器插件

The extension connects only to 127.0.0.1 on your machine using a pairing token shown in desktop Settings. It does not read arbitrary files or send data to third parties.

插件仅通过设置页显示的配对 Token 连接本机 127.0.0.1，不读取任意文件，不向第三方发送数据。

Updates / 更新

When you check for updates, the app may contact GitHub Releases to download installers.

检查更新时，应用可能访问 GitHub Releases 以下载安装包。
