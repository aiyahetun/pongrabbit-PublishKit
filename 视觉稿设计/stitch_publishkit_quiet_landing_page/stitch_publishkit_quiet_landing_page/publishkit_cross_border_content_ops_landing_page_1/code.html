Our Philosophy

The Quiet Tool Manifesto

Why we built PublishKit as a local desktop tool rather than another bloated SaaS subscription.

What We Do

  • Store all drafts & media strictly on your local disk in standard SQLite.
  • Respect platform integrity — you remain logged in natively with zero bot shadowbans.
  • Offer single-purchase lifetime licenses with continuous utility updates.
  • Provide lightning-fast instant keyboard shortcuts (Cmd+K, J/K navigation).
  • Design for silence: no notification badges, no gamified streaks, no upsell popups.

What We Don't Do

  • Never ask for your account passwords, cookies, or OAuth management tokens.
  • Never proxy your sensitive posts or IP addresses through untrusted shared servers.
  • Never trap your creative portfolio in a monthly recurring subscription tax.
  • Never inject AI hype text without your explicit local prompt.
  • Never sell aggregate analytics or post impressions to advertising data brokers.
